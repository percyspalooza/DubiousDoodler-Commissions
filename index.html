Terms of service

Payment & scheduling
  • All payments are processed through VGen.
  • For most pieces, payment is due once you approve the sketch in the proposal.
  • Experimental PWYW pieces and YCHs are paid upfront, before work begins.
  • Turnaround time is about 4 weeks.
  • If payment isn't sent, or I don't hear back from you for 24 to 48 hours at any point, your slot is revoked. For a custom character, this may mean it gets resold.
  • False chargebacks are not tolerated and will be disputed.
WIPs, changes & refunds
  • You'll get WIPs at least at sketch, cleaned lines and finished. I get sketch approval before moving on to lineart and color.
  • Prefer fewer check-ins? Just say so and I'll send only the finished piece.
  • If it's been quiet for a while, feel free to poke me for a status check, or check the queue.
  • Major alterations (pose changes, major expression changes, etc.) aren't accepted once lineart has started. Smaller changes are still fine after sketch approval.
  • Refunds aren't offered, except if I'm unable to complete the commission, or there's a delay beyond a reasonable timeframe caused solely by me without communication.
Usage & rights
  • I do not consent to my art being used for AI generation or AI training, NFTs or other blockchain-related technology, tracing, or hateful or discriminatory messaging. No exceptions.
  • I retain full rights to the artwork; you retain rights to your character.
  • I can decline any commission request without needing to explain why.
  • Only you and I may repost the piece, for non-commercial use, with credit: character credit to you, art credit to me, @Dubious.Doodler.
  • Don't edit, alter or modify the artwork without my explicit permission. Cropping for an icon or adjusting the background for a layout is fine.
  • Don't repost, redistribute or claim my artwork as your own.
  • If the character turns out to be stolen, pirated, or not owned by or commissioned for you, you'll be blacklisted and reported.
